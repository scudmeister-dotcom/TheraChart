Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

TheraChart is an electronic medical record for physical therapy clinics. This page explains, in plain language, what information the service holds, where it is kept, who can reach it, and what leaves the clinic.

The short version.
  • A clinic's records belong to that clinic. We hold them on the clinic's behalf.
  • No clinic can see another clinic's records. Neither can we, through the application.
  • There are no cookies, no advertising and no third-party analytics anywhere in the product.
  • Dictation audio is discarded as soon as it is transcribed, unless a clinic deliberately turns on consented review.
  • A clinic can export everything, or erase everything, without asking us.

Who we are

TheraChart is operated by Amador Moriles. For any question about this policy, or about information the service holds, write to amador.moriles@gmail.com.

When a clinic uses TheraChart to keep patient records, the clinic decides what is recorded and why; we process that information on the clinic's instructions in order to provide the service.

What the service holds

Information a clinic enters

  • Patient records — identity and contact details, clinical history, examination findings and measurements, visit notes, plans of care, appointments, billing lines, and any files the clinic uploads such as referrals or imaging.
  • Staff accounts — name, email address, role, and professional licence number and expiry where the role requires one.

Information created by using the service

  • An activity log — sign-ins, charts opened, notes signed and amendments made, with who did it and when. This exists so a clinic can answer "who opened this chart?".
  • Dictation audio and transcripts — see Voice dictation.
  • Usage counts — tokens and audio seconds consumed, so a clinic can see what it has spent. These are counts, not content.

If you sign in with Google

Google returns your email address, name and Google account identifier. We use them only to identify you and to match you to an approved account. We do not receive your Google password, and we ask for no access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google service — the sign-in requests basic profile information and nothing more.

Where the information is kept

TheraChart runs on Google Cloud, under a data protection agreement we have signed and hold, covering every service a record touches: Cloud Run, Cloud SQL, Cloud Storage, Speech-to-Text and Vertex AI. That agreement binds Google as a processor and limits what the data may be used for.

  • Records live in an encrypted managed PostgreSQL database, encrypted in transit and at rest, reachable only by this application and never directly over the internet.
  • Uploaded files are kept in Google Cloud Storage and are streamed back only to a signed-in member of the owning clinic.
  • Unless a clinic arranged otherwise at setup, the application and database run in a United States region. For a clinic in the Philippines this means records are stored and processed outside the country. A Singapore region is available instead; a clinic administrator can confirm which one applies.

Keeping clinics apart

Every record carries the clinic it belongs to, and every read is filtered by the signed-in user's clinic on the server — not hidden in the page, where a determined browser could work around it. There is no account, including ours, that can open another clinic's charts through the application.

Voice dictation

When a clinician dictates, the audio streams to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, which returns text. It is a paid Google Cloud service under the agreement above — not a consumer speech service.

  • By default the audio is held only in memory and discarded once it has been transcribed. Only the transcript is saved to the chart.
  • Silence is dropped on the device before anything is sent.
  • A clinic may turn on session-audio review, which is off by default. It briefly keeps dictation audio, only for patients who have consented, so a clinician can replay it to check the transcript. Kept audio is deleted when the note is signed, or at the end of the clinic's retention window, whichever comes first. The patient's consent is recorded in their chart.

Artificial intelligence

Where a clinic has AI features enabled, pressing Review & clean up, asking for insights, using the patient assistant, or importing a scanned document sends the text — never the audio — to Google Gemini on Vertex AI.

  • That text can include a patient's name and clinical detail. It is health information being sent out of the clinic's region for processing, and we say so plainly rather than bury it.
  • Under Google's Vertex AI service terms, customer prompts and responses are not used to train Google's models and are not sold.
  • The AI only ever suggests. Nothing enters a chart until a licensed clinician has reviewed it and signed.
  • Where no AI is connected, no clinical text leaves the clinic for AI processing at all, and those features are switched off rather than approximated.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use patient information for advertising.
  • We do not use a clinic's records to train our own models.
  • There are no cookies. A signed-in session is a token held in your own browser's storage, which is cleared when you sign out.
  • There is no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising code in the product. No Google Analytics, no tag managers, no social pixels, no session recorders.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signs in with their own account and sees only what their role needs.
  • Expired licences and voided accounts lose access automatically.
  • Signed documents lock. A later change needs a signed, authorised amendment, and the original stays readable.
  • Notable actions, including reads, are written to the clinic's activity log.
  • Our own access is limited to what is needed to operate and support the service, and we do not open clinical records except at a clinic's request or where the law requires it.

How long information is kept

Clinical records are kept for as long as the clinic keeps using the service, because a medical record is meant to persist — retention periods for health records are set by the clinic's own regulator, not by us. A clinic can erase everything at any time. Dictation audio is the exception and is deleted as described above.

A clinic's control over its own data

  • Export backup takes the whole record set out in an open format.
  • Erase all data removes it.

Neither needs our permission and neither is throttled. There is no lock-in.

Individual rights

If you are a patient and want to know what a clinic holds about you, to correct it, or to have it erased, contact the clinic that treated you — the records are theirs and they are the ones who can act on the request. We will support them in doing so. Depending on where you live, laws such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) give you those rights directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how information is handled, the date at the top changes and clinic administrators are told. A privacy page that has drifted out of date is worse than none, so we would rather over-communicate.
