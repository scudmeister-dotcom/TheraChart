2. What TheraChart is — and what it is not

TheraChart records, organises and helps write clinical documentation. It also offers optional features that use artificial intelligence to transcribe speech, tidy a transcript into a note, summarise a chart, and read scanned documents.

TheraChart does not practise medicine. It does not diagnose, does not prescribe, does not decide a plan of care, and is not a substitute for professional judgement. Every AI feature is decision support for a licensed clinician. Nothing produced by the software enters a patient's chart until a licensed clinician has read it, corrected it where needed, and signed it. Clinical responsibility for what is recorded and what is done rests entirely with the clinician and the clinic.
